# OpenDental Atlas 🦷

**An Open-Source Dental and Maxillofacial Knowledge Platform**

OpenDental Atlas is an open-source educational project designed to organize and make dental, oral pathology, maxillofacial surgery, CBCT imaging, and implantology knowledge accessible to students and dental professionals.

## 🌍 Languages
- English
- Uzbek

## 📚 Core Modules

1. **Oral Anatomy** — Dental and maxillofacial anatomy.
2. **Oral Pathology** — Cysts, tumors, and inflammatory diseases.
3. **Maxillofacial Surgery** — Surgical procedures, trauma, and reconstruction.
4. **CBCT Atlas** — Radiographic anatomy and diagnostic education.
5. **Implantology** — Implant planning, bone quality, and surgical principles.

## 🎯 Mission

To build an accessible, structured, evidence-informed educational resource for the global dental community.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

OpenDental Atlas is an educational resource. It does not replace professional clinical judgment, formal medical training, or patient-specific diagnosis and treatment.
